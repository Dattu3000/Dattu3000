Full Description:

Crictrack is your ultimate companion for tracking local cricket matches. Designed specifically with a sleek, dark-themed, mobile-first interface, it allows you to easily track ball-by-ball scores without needing an active internet connection.

Features Include: • Offline Support: Score your matches anywhere, your data saves right to your local device. • Flexible Match Setup: Play short 5-over box cricket games up to full 50-over ODIs. • Comprehensive Scoring: Track runs, wides, no-balls, leg-byes, and detailed wicket types. • Smart Player Management: Easily manage striker rotation and substitute fielders. • Match History & Standings: Keep a permanent record of all your past games and tournaments. • Export Scorecards: Instantly generate and share beautiful match summaries with your team.

Leave the pen and paper behind and bring your local games into the digital age with Crictrack!
